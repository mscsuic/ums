About the symposium

The Undergraduate Mathematics Symposium at UIC is an annual one-day meeting focusing on undergraduate mathematical research and education. The meeting features plenary lectures by mathematical researchers and a poster session where students present results from undergraduate mathematical research projects.

Download the 2026 symposium poster (PDF)

The next UMS will be held on November 14, 2026. It will feature a poster session for undergraduate researchers and two invited plenary lectures by mathematical researchers. Information about applying to present a poster can be found below.

Plenary speakers

  • Jayadev Athreya (Fordham University)
    Title: Bouncing around: coding billiards in polygons
    Abstract: How many (combinatorial) ways can a billiard ball bounce around a table? We answer this seemingly simple question when the billiard table is a regular polygon, in joint work with Pascal Hubert and Serge Troubetzkoy. A theme of this talk will be the fact that we in fact know very little about general mathematical billiards in polygons, even though they are very simple models of Newtonian mechanics. There will be lots of pictures, and introductions to many fun mathematical objects, including translation surfaces, group actions on moduli spaces, and in the end, trigonometric identities.

Poster session

Undergraduates who have completed an REU, summer program, or other supervised mathematical research experience can apply to present a poster at UMS.

The deadline to request travel funding has now passed. There is no fixed deadline to apply to present a poster without requesting funding.

All poster presenters must also complete the participant registration form.

Attending the symposium

Everyone is welcome to attend. There is no fixed registration deadline, but please register so we can plan for the correct number of participants. Poster presenters must register as well as submit a poster application.
